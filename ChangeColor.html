Skincare is the process of caring for and maintaining the health of your skin. It involves keeping the skin clean, hydrated, and protected from damage caused by factors such as sunlight, pollution, and harsh weather conditions. A skincare routine may include cleansing, moisturizing, and applying sunscreen, as well as using products designed to address specific concerns like acne, dryness, dark spots, or signs of aging. Proper skincare helps the skin remain healthy, smooth, and radiant while supporting its natural protective barrier.
